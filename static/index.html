Tuning

Sheets are transcribed by ear from the separated track, so treat them as a draft to check against the recording. They're written in 4/4 on a 16th-note grid, so triplets and other time signatures will come out approximate. Clean, single-instrument tracks work best. Every view plays back with a synth, not the recording. Guitar Pro shows notation and tab together. Tab frets are for the tuning you pick; notation always shows the notes as they sound.
